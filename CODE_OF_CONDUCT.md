# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to work on, whatever your background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and can block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or a direct message on the maintainer's profile.
